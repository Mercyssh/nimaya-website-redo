About us

Every Naari deserves an equal Start

Nimaya is a not-for-profit founded in 2021 by Navya Naveli Nanda and Samyak Chakrabarty on a simple belief: a young woman’s future should not be limited by her background. We prepare women from India’s most underserved communities for the AI-era workplace, with GenAI skills, industry exposure and a path into their first jobs.

Since 2021

A New Template for Change

Every woman we train builds more than a skill. She changes what’s possible for her family and for the girls who come after her.

India spends crores every year on vocational training built for jobs that are disappearing. Nimaya offers CSR leaders, philanthropists and policymakers a different model: future-focused, measurable and designed for the AI economy.

Our Mission

To ensure every Nimaya Naari begins her journey on equal footing with her most privileged peers.

  • Skills: GenAI and the human skills AI can’t replace
  • Exposure: real projects, mentors and workplaces
  • Access: credits, certificates and placement support
