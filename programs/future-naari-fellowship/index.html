The Future Ready Naari Fellowship

A 12-month, placement-linked fellowship that develops ambitious young women into future leaders, through 1:1 mentoring with senior women leaders, masterclasses and industry projects.

What is the Fellowship

A 12-month program that gives young women the exposure, skills and network to build high-growth careers in an AI-shaped economy, and to stand out for promotions and meaningful work.

It is designed for women with the drive to lead, who lack the networks and exposure their more privileged peers take for granted.

Through our flagship programs we have already enabled 10,000+ Nimaya Naaris across 6 states to build future-ready careers.

FAQs

Who is the fellowship for?

Young women in college in Mumbai with strong potential but limited exposure or opportunities. We look for curiosity, ambition and a readiness to grow.

Do fellows need prior work experience or technical skills?

Not at all. We’re not looking for perfect resumes; we’re looking for curious, ambitious minds and a willingness to learn. The fellowship builds confidence, skills and workplace readiness from scratch.

How long is the fellowship?

It’s a 12-month immersive journey, packed with:

  • 1:1 mentorship
  • Masterclasses
  • 6-month live industry projects
  • GenAI learning
  • The Capstone Conclave
  • Career preparation and placement support
Is it online or offline?

It’s hybrid: virtual learning sessions, with select in-person workshops, meetups and the final Capstone Conclave.

How much time does it take each week?

Around 4–6 hours a week, including mentoring, masterclasses, project work and self-learning. It’s designed to be challenging but manageable alongside college or early work.

What mentorship do fellows receive?

Each fellow is paired with a senior woman leader (a CXO, founder or experienced professional) who mentors her through monthly 1:1 sessions across the year.

Is there a fee or a stipend?

There is no fee: the fellowship is fully funded by our partners, because money should never limit potential. It doesn’t offer a stipend, but comes with strong placement support, mentoring and deep industry exposure.

Do fellows receive a certificate?

Yes. Fellows who complete the program receive a Certificate of Completion, along with professional exposure and project credentials.
