The GenAI Ready Naari Program

A free, 40-hour hybrid program that equips young women with GenAI tools and the workplace skills employers now hire for, delivered with NGO, college and CSR partners.

  • 40 hoursHybrid learning
  • NSQF creditAdded to your ABC on passing
  • ₹0Absolutely no fees
  • Placement& training support

How Naaris Learn

The program is built on practice, not memorisation. From the first session, Naaris experiment, build and apply what they learn to real problems.

  • Interactive sessions

    Ten live, two-hour sessions led by trainers who make every concept practical.

  • Hands-on with GenAI

    Naaris prompt, create, research and automate with the AI tools workplaces use today.

  • Live projects

    Real briefs from leading companies and MNCs, not classroom exercises.

  • Learning together

    Group work, peer feedback and a community of Naaris who push each other forward.

  • “Smart Didi” AI coach

    Our AI-powered LMS keeps learning going between sessions, with a bot that coaches each Naari for job success.

  • Assessed & certified

    A final assessment leads to an NSQF-aligned academic credit and a recognised certificate.

Beyond the Classroom

Now part of the IndiaAI National AI Literacy Program

GenAI Ready Naari is part of the Government of India’s IndiaAI initiative. Every Naari who completes the program earns a recognised certificate carrying the IndiaAI logo.

#NationalAILiteracyProgram

Earn an NSQF-aligned academic credit

The program carries a 30-hour credit under the National Skills Qualifications Framework, governed by NCVET and awarded by Jindal Steel. Learners who complete the 30 hours and pass the assessment have the credit added automatically to their Academic Bank of Credits (ABC) via their APAAR ID, under the NEP.

Who Is This For

Young women preparing for entry-level roles in HR, finance, IT, sales, operations or data, where GenAI and workplace skills are now essential. We select women who:

  • Look beyond degrees

    Believe that degrees and technical skills alone aren’t enough.

  • Are ambitious

    Want to deliver great work and make an impact.

  • Are resilient

    Come from difficult social or financial circumstances, yet are determined to succeed.

  • Are committed

    Can attend 100% of the online and offline sessions.

  • Are getting ready for work

    Are in their second or final year of college, or recent graduates.

  • Speak English comfortably

    Can communicate in English and have access to a smartphone.
