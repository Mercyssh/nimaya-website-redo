Meet Maya

Our agentic AI who runs the backend, so our team can focus on what matters: our Naaris!

What if an NGO never forgot a single thing a Naari told it? That is Maya. She is the agentic AI at the heart of Nimaya, and she listens, learns and joins the dots across every classroom, city and cohort. What a Naari tells Maya on a call today shapes tomorrow’s session, next month’s curriculum and even how our newest teammate is trained. Nothing sits in a silo.

And our people? They do what only people can: show up for our Naaris!
