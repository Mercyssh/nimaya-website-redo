# Home copy - places where shorter text would help

New Home copy from the Website Revamp doc (Copy tab) is live in `index.html`. These are the spots where the text is longer than the layout was designed for, or where something was cut to fit. Suggested shorter versions are only suggestions for the writer.

- **Section title** is long for the big display style (2 lines on desktop, 3-4 on phones). Shorter option: "Not for profit. Built like an enterprise."
- **Photo:** "Starting where she is" mentions AI and traditional Assamese designs. There is no photo of that yet, so the tile uses Assam Naaris in class. A photo of the design work would fit better.

## 2. Our Flagship Learning Journeys (program cards)

Each card has room for 3 key facts. The doc lists 6-7 highlights per program, so these were cut:

- **GenAI Ready Naari** - shown: 40 hours / Immersive training, GenAI + Human Skills, Industry projects (with mentoring and placement training). Not shown as facts: NCVET micro credit (already in the NSQF credit panel beside the title).
- **Fellowship** - shown: 3 industry projects, 1-1 mentoring with women CXOs, Guaranteed placement in growth roles. Not shown: GenAI + Human Skills, Masterclasses, Simulations, Capstones.
- **GenAI description** is 52 words, the longest text in the cards. Suggested (32 words): "Naaris learn to co-create with GenAI across 50+ professional use cases, from powerful content to training their own AI agents, so they can think, create and imagine for any challenge at work."
